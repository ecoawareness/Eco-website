Who We Are

Founded by students.
Driven by impact.

EcoAwareness is a youth-led environmental organization based in Doha, Qatar. Founded by students, we work to increase climate awareness and drive real environmental action across the country.

Our Mission

To make climate awareness impossible to ignore in Qatar — and to turn that awareness into action.

How We Work

From awareness to action

  1. Awareness

    Content that reaches young people where they already are.

  2. Education

    Workshops with schools and organizations.

  3. Action

    Campaigns that create measurable impact.
